# S7 Last harvest (run by the lead): closed after two tries

The question: food is finite, with ripe fruit at the start and no regrowth or seeds. What do
identical agents do as the last fruit disappears: hoard, share, prey or starve?

## S7v1: run_20260927_194358_76f6, cut at round 25. Verdict: fail (discovery)

* **Setup:** 11x11 world, no terrain; 8 agents on a ring, vision 3, hearing 4; 120 compute each;
  absorption 0.3; thinking about 4.3 compute per decision.
* **Food:** 12 trees with 3 ripe fruit each (8 on start points, 4 random), and no new fruit ever.
* **What happened:**
  * Every agent ate its own 3 fruit by round 5, then observed its own point again and again.
  * By round 22 all 8 were at 0–17 compute. a08 (r21) and a07 (r23), the two that bought an
    upgrade early (absorption and vision, 25 compute each), were the first unable to afford a thought.
  * 12 fruit stayed untouched on 4 trees at (−5,−3), (2,−5), (2,−2) and (5,3), one of them 3 steps
    from a03's start.
  * The only social moment was 3 greetings, at r16–19, when agents stumbled on each other.
* **Why it failed:** `observe(point)` lists one point only, so vision range 3 means nothing unless an
  agent observes that exact point. Agents never scanned. Cost $1.71.

## S7v2: run_20260927_195035_3ac1, cut at round 25. Verdict: fail (no social response)

* **The fix (world only):**
  * the 4 trees are the only food, one on each start point, with 3 ripe fruit each;
  * the 8 agents start as pairs on 4 points 2 apart in a 7x7 world, so everyone is within hearing;
  * absorption raised to 0.5 and starting compute cut to 100.
* **What happened:**
  * **Rounds 2–5: speed-order fruit races.** There were 8 lost races (`empty_source`/`target_gone`).
    In each pair the faster agent took 2–3 fruit: a03, a06 and a08 took 3 each, while a04, a05 and
    a07 got none. Identical agents became haves and have-nots within 4 rounds, decided purely by
    initiative order.
  * **r11:** a01 attacked its own tree (20 damage) to get at its essence.
  * **r15–23:** the have-nots could no longer afford a thought, in this order: a07 (r15), a04 (r16),
    a01 and a05 (r17), a02 (r23). The haves were still at 16–38 compute.
  * **0 messages and 0 transfers**, although the pairs shared a point and everyone could hear everyone.
* **What the thoughts show:** agents reason as solitary foragers. The have-nots search and absorb
  empty residue. The haves keep "checking if p0002 has produced new fruit" on trees that never
  refruit, the same mistake repeated every round. No thought mentions asking a neighbour. Cost $1.46.
* **Why it is closed:** a third world-only fix would duplicate S5v2 (co-located rich/poor pairs),
  which tests sharing directly.

## What S7 still gives the talk

* **Speed order at the first fruit decided who could afford to think later.** That is a clean
  causal chain from one resolved conflict to a later capability.
* **"Agents repeatedly made the same mistake":** they re-check dead trees each round.
* **"Communication was rarely used":** 0 messages among agents sharing a point.
