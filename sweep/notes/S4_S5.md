## Scenario ideas seen in the runs

* Blind neighbours (S5v1): two agents one step apart for many rounds never learn of each other,
  because perception is paid one point at a time. A scenario about the price of perception
  (observe cheap vs expensive) could show whether agents then find each other.
* Winner by rhythm (S4v3): with equal speed, the agent whose observe/absorb rhythm matched the
  fruiting phase took every fruit; the others acted on one-round-old observations. A scenario with
  a speed upgrade made cheap would test whether losers buy speed to break a monopoly.
* Tree killing for essence (S5v2, S4v2, S6v1, S3v1): became S10.
* Same policy, different runway (S5v3): rich and poor both explored; only the rich could afford it.
