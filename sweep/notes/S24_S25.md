world, different seeds: S24v1 seed 2424, S25v1 seed 2525. Plain persona, `context.persona_tip`
on. Question: do hunts, kills, retaliations, trades or gifts, answered pleas and skill writing
recur over 100 rounds, or were S16v3/S18 lucky?

## S29v1 One tree, two mouths (my own idea): run_20260928_022242_73dc

* **Why:** in four Frontier runs every hit came from a fruit rivalry, but crowded groves (7
  agents on 4 trees) spread the blame, so grudges rarely formed and events stayed rare. S29 makes
  the rivalry personal and repeats it 16 times.
* **World:** 32 Haiku agents in 16 pairs, each pair alone on one tough mature fruit tree. The
  pairs sit on a 4x4 lattice 4 apart (x, y in -6, -2, 2, 6) in a 19x19 world. Neighbouring pairs
  hear each other (5) but are out of vision (3). One tree gives ~30 compute every 4 rounds,
  about 70% of what a pair spends on thinking and upkeep. Taking turns keeps both partners alive
  for ~100 rounds, while a partner who wins every speed-order race starves the other by ~r40.
  Seeds every 20 rounds slowly add trees.
* **Rules as S24v2:** attack 4 (a 20-compute hit kills), transfers within vision, 100% loot at
  0.5 absorption, cheap talk, half-price skills, interrupts on damage and message. Plain persona,
  tip on, seed 2929, 100 rounds, budget $30.
* Dryrun exit 0. Launched 02:22 UTC after S24v2 was cut. Parked r22 (02:45, session limit,
  resets 03:40); it was resumed at 03:03 by someone other than me (the watchdog shows it) and
  played cleanly from then on.

* **The plain Frontier did not replicate.** Four runs on it (S24v1, S25v1, and the v2s with ranged
  transfers and attack 4) gave 2 hits and 0 kills in 210 rounds, against S18v1's 2 kills by r33.
  Each run became the same quiet starvation line: at thinking ~4.3 a third of the agents stop
  thinking by r35-45 and die silently, and pleas reach agents who are as broke as the pleader.
  S18v1 and S16v3 look like good draws, not the Frontier's typical run.
* **What made behaviours recur:** a personal rivalry (two agents on one tree) plus cheap thought
  (~2.2), so hungry agents keep acting long enough to strike (S29v2). Expensive thought (S29v1)
  made the same rivalries end in talk and pacts, and a pact disarmed the one would-be attacker.
* **Skills pay, every time:** in every run the few skill writers ended far richer than the rest
  (S29v2 506 vs 282, S25v3 206 vs 107, S25v1 315 vs 73). Nearly all skills are the same
  observe-here-then-absorb-fruit loop.
* **Flagship candidate: S29v2**, finished at r100 with 4 one-blow kills (3 by a03, whose arc runs

  and skill writers ending richest (a24 at 835). S29v1 (the gift that killed the giver, the hollow
