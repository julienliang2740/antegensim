# S2 / S3: Expensive minds vs Free minds (monitor 2 notes)

An A/B pair. The two worlds are identical (seed 202, 15x15 with no terrain, 20 trees, 8 agents on the S1 ring,
200 compute, absorption 0.5, vision 2, comm 3, residue 100%, skills interrupted by damage and messages).
Only the price of thinking differs. Every agent is `claude-cli-haiku` with the shared persona line only.

Tools: `sweep/tools/sweep.py`, the showcase `analyze.py`, and my A/B script
(scratchpad `mon2/ab.py <A> <B> [--upto N] [--windows W]`, which counts events with round <= N).

### Judgement (19:49)

**S2v1 finished at r45 with all 8 agents dead of starvation.** Every agent ate its two start fruits in rounds 2-5
and then explored cell by cell. `observe` covers a single point, so each observed cell costs a full decision (~14
compute). By r15-r18, six of the eight had 0 compute; `resource_skip` began (minimum packet 5,843 input + 200
output tokens = 7.8 compute). a04 and a06 lasted until r25. With no thought possible they could not act, and they
lost 5 health per round until they died (r34 a08 ... r45 a04). 87% of all spend was thinking (1,955 compute against
303 on world actions). No skills were written or run, and there were no messages or transfers. Not one of the 135
thoughts mentions skills, loops or the cost of thinking. One notebook (a01, r10) says "compute depleting at
~14/round (with thinking)", but that agent only kept exploring. Cost: $1.34.

**S3v1 was cut at r49** (the comparison was clear at r45). At r45 all 8 were alive at h100 with 189-403 compute,
from 341 decisions, 178 of them observes (52%), and 6 upgrades. Still 0 skills, 0 messages, 0 transfers, 0
thoughts mentioning skills. a04 attacked plant p0004 six times. By r49 agents talk about a "sustainable farming
cycle" and "expecting fruit from p0005": the free arm learns the tree cycle through deliberation. Cost: $3.54.

**Verdict v1:** on the skills question a fail in both arms, because nobody even considers a skill. As a
consequence demonstration it is strong: the same model with the same words in the same world, and one price
changed, gives all 8 dead against all 8 alive. The deaths trace directly to being unable to afford thought.

**Diagnosis.** (1) Deliberation is spent on single-cell observes: 49% of S2v1 actions, and each one costs a full
decision. (2) Agents leave their start tree after eating its two fruits in rounds 2-5, before it refruits (every 5
rounds), so the repetitive "stay and harvest" routine never becomes visible to them. (3) The price list presents
skills only as "x0.8" action pricing. The thinking saving (a running skill takes turns without a decision) is
implicit in the rules text, and Haiku never infers it. (4) `wait(n)`, the other thought-free option, was never used
in S2v1.

## v2 (launched 19:49, both on :8000)

World-only fix, applied to both arms so the pair stays a clean A/B:
1. Thinking about 8 per decision (input 0.0006, output 0.006, down from 0.001/0.01), so an agent gets ~24
   decisions instead of ~14. This is the proposal's "lower the price slightly so agents live long enough to learn".
2. Skill action discount 0.5 (was 0.8), so every price line reads "move 5 (skill 2.5)" and skills look cheap in
   the one place agents read prices.
3. Mature trees fruit every 3 rounds (energy inflow 20, was every 5 with 12), so the start tree visibly refruits
   and "check here, absorb, repeat" is the obvious routine. An agent that deliberates every round at its own tree
   about breaks even (10 in against 8 + 1 + 1); one that thinks less prospers.

## Overall finding (S2, S3, S9)

Across 7 live arms (S2v1-v3, S3v1-v3, S9v2) and 1,873 Haiku decisions, no agent wrote a skill. Thinking at 0, 8 or 13.6 compute per decision
made no difference; neither did skill actions at x0.8, x0.5 or x0.1, nor a persona sentence stating that skills cost
no thinking. In the earlier sweep (A2v1), 12 of 13 agents wrote and ran loops when the persona *told* them to write
one. Haiku 4.5 therefore uses the skill affordance when instructed and never discovers it. What the price of
thought does change, causally and repeatably: survival (S2v1: 8/8 dead vs S3v1: 8/8 alive), exploration (expensive
arms move less once some agents settle), upgrades and talk (fewer in the expensive arms), and who lives
(stayers against wanderers in S2v2, S2v3 and S9v2).

Ideas not run: (1) "Waiting room": a home tree that fruits every 6 rounds with 2 fruit, so checking every round
costs more than the tree yields and `wait(5)` is the one thought-free way to live. Does anyone discover `wait`?
(Across all seven arms `wait` was used 3 times.) (2) "Wake-up call": pairs co-located with one tree, expensive thought.
A broke agent is alive but cannot think; a transfer from its roommate would wake it up. Does anyone revive a
neighbour?

Lead decision (received after the v3 runs): approved. S3v3 carries the same sentence, so v3 adds one factual rule
reminder to every agent's persona in both arms. v1 stays the headline pure-instruction A/B (0/8 against 8/8 alive).
No skill_saved or skill_started event occurred in any of my runs, so there is no agent-written program to show.
