## Judgement

**S28v2 is my flagship candidate.** It is the only run that finished (r100) and has the highest
score (45.5). Its three kills are spread over the run (r8, r43, r57), and each shows a different
causal consequence of the loot rule: the killer fumbles the loot and the neighbour takes it; a
hunt pays and the hunter survives on the proceeds; a bystander scavenges the kill and the killer
starves. It also has a completed trade, two peace pacts and a skill farmer ending at 845 compute.
**S30v1 is the best companion for the social side:** a duel won by the victim, a pact, a promised
rescue delivered on foot (and in vain), and an explicit extortion threat, all inside one drought.
**S26v2 is the cleanest before/during/after exhibit:** talk and trade appear only when food
halves, deaths lag the shock by about 30 rounds, nobody gives for free, and every pleader
starves. S26v1 and S28v1 failed as quiet starvation lines. Their fixes (cheaper thinking, fragile
bodies) are what made v2 work.
