## S11v1 Cheap talk: run_20260927_200138_7be5, finished r60, 3 of 8 alive. Verdict: middling (a clean negative)

* **Question:** is talk rare because it costs, or because the agents do not think of it?
* **Setup:**
  * 13x13 world with no terrain; 8 identical agents on a ring.
  * 16 trees at seeded random points, none on a start point, 1 ripe fruit each.
  * vision 2 and **communication range 20** (everyone hears everyone).
  * **send and broadcast cost 0.5** (defaults 3 and 7).
  * 150 compute, absorption 0.4, thinking about 4.3 per decision.
* **What happened:**
  * **Rounds 1–13: 0 messages.** a07 found a tree and ate 4 fruit alone.
  * **r14:** a05 broadcast "Running low on compute (critical). Seeking contact with other agents for
    trade/cooperation or resource information."
  * **r17:** a06 broadcast "16 rounds of exploration found zero resources ... Compute critically low
    (~48). Seeking: (1) resource location info, (2) trade opportunities". Nobody answered, including
    the agents who had found food.
  * **r19–30:** a03 attacked a tree (for essence) twice, and a07 attacked a tree at r30.
  * **r21–24:** the searchers a04, a03, a06 and a05 could no longer afford to think.
  * **End:** 3 alive at r60.
* **Reading:** with talk almost free and global, there were 2 messages in 60 rounds, both cries for
  help from starving agents. Food locations were never shared. Rare talk here is a disposition of
  the model, not a price effect.
* Cost: see the report table.

## S12v1 Told about skills: run_20260927_201617_1475, finished r50, 8 of 8 alive. Verdict: success (skill emergence with a tip)

* **Question:** after 1,873 Haiku decisions with no skill written (S2, S3, S9), do Haiku agents write
  their own programs once the idea is named? And does it pay?
* **Setup:**
  * the S2v2 world exactly: 15x15 with no terrain, 20 trees, trees refruit every 3 rounds;
  * thinking about 8 compute per decision; skill actions at x0.5;
  * 200 compute, absorption 0.5.
* **The one change:** every persona adds this tip, which names the mechanism but not what to write:
  "Tip: a routine you find yourself repeating can be saved as a skill (save_skills) and started with
  run_skill; while it runs you make no decisions, so you pay no thinking. Whether and what to
  automate is your call."
* **Timeline:**
  * r11 a02 saves `gather_fruit`; r12 runs it.
  * r17 a06 saves `harvest_wait` and runs it at once. It absorbs every fruit on its point, then
    `wait(2)`, which matches the tree's 3-round refruit cycle; this is its own variation on the example.
  * r25–27 a01 saves `gather_routine` and a07 saves `gather_fruit`, and both run them.
  * a03, a04, a05 and a08 never write a skill and deliberate every round (48–50 decisions each).
  * 0 messages: the four writers found the idea independently, at rounds 11, 17, 25 and 26.
* **Outcome (r50):**

  | | writers a01, a02, a06, a07 | non-writers a03, a04, a05, a08 |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | fruit eaten | 17–19 | 15–18 |
  | decisions | 23–39 | 48–50 |
  | thinking bill (compute) | 217–298 (mean 267) | 397–435 (mean 410) |
  | compute at r50 (started 200) | 294–398 (mean 355) | 59–208 (mean 160) |

  The same food went in; the difference is the thinking bill. Skills carried 30% of all actions
  (112 of 377) and there were 62 skill runs. Cost $3.15.
