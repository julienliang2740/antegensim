# Category E: balanced stories (sweep-E)

Shared design (all E scenarios, generated by scratchpad `sweepE/gen_e*.py`):
* Trees sit on the first N distinct agent start cells (card order), so groves are placed exactly; seeds chosen so no
  card or tree lands on a mountain/water (checked by building the round-0 world in-process).
* Economy: compute_absorption 0.5 (a 60-compute fruit gives 30), essence_absorption 0.5, upkeep 1.5, starvation 8
  health/round, cognition x1.5 (input 0.0003, generation 0.0015), move 2, observe/query 0.5, send 2, broadcast 5,
  absorb 2. Residue 70% compute and essence. query finds any entity anywhere (query_uses_vision_range false),
  attacks need the same cell, interrupt_on damage.
* Seeded skills (verified with a fake-scripted in-process test): step, goto(point), harvest(n) (observe cell,
  absorb fruit/residue, wait 2 when empty), raid(target, blow) (query-chase, attack on same cell, loot on kill).
* Personas carry the exact world facts (grove cells, ids, food maths) plus a role.

## v1 designs (launched 2026-09-27 ~04:50)
* E1v1 Frontier valley (30: 27 Haiku + 3 Sonnet, 100 rounds, 41x41). North Orchard 5 trees / West Grove 3 / East
  Thicket 2 with 6/4/3 farmers (140 compute); Crag raiders (4, Sonnet warlord) and Dunes raiders (5), attack 2, 110
  compute, no trees; 8 traders (Sonnet guild master) with 45 essence and no food. Supply ~60/round vs demand ~100.
* E2v1 Famine clock (24: 22 Haiku + 2 Sonnet elders, 100 rounds, 25x25). 4 hamlets x 3 trees x 6 agents. Custom tree
  stages: bloom (fruit every 4 rounds) to r15, failing (every 15) to r25, famine (1.5 energy/round) after; no seeds.
  Start compute 50, attack 1.5. The calendar is in every persona. Roles: elder/hoarder/guard/sharer/scout/opportunist.
* E3v1 Three houses (30: 27 Haiku + 3 Sonnet lords, 100 rounds, 37x37). Heartwood 6 trees held by 6 free wanderers
  (attack 2, 100 compute) in the centre; houses Vael/Morrow/Sarn with 8 members on 2 trees each (lord Sonnet comm 12,
  2 knights attack 2, steward, envoy, forager, spy, heir).
* E4v1 The tyrant (21: 1 Sonnet + 20 Haiku, 80 rounds, 25x25). Tyrant 600 compute, 200 hp, attack 3 cap 100 on the
  6-tree Iron Grove with 5 thralls; peasants (70 compute, 110 hp, attack 1) at two lone trees and a treeless camp.
* E5v1 Settlers meet wanderers (24 Haiku, 100 rounds, 31x31). 8-tree Stillwater with 12 settlers (200 compute, 4
  essence); 12 wanderers (55 compute, 40 essence, attack 1.5) 10 cells east.

## Judgements
* 05:20 E1v1, E3v1, E4v1, E5v1 CUT at r5-r13: a setup bug. The personas (~2600 tokens in stable rules) plus the Haiku
  CLI overhead (~2700) pushed the mandatory packet over input_token_cap 6000 once a cell was crowded, so those agents
  got resource_skip on every turn (E4: 6 peasants locked out from r3). The behaviour before the cut looked healthy:
  skills 41-93% of actions, tribute broadcasts, wanderers marching.
  v2 = the same design with context.input_token_cap 10000 (E1v2, E3v2, E4v2, E5v2 launched 05:21).
* E2v1 kept: 0 resource skips at r11 (less crowded cells).
